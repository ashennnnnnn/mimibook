My Mimi

She is water to the parched wanderer,
an oasis shimmering in the desert’s cruel silence,
each breath of hers a sip of salvation,
each glance a wellspring of life renewed.

She is the light at tunnel’s end,
a beacon piercing the suffocating dark,
her presence the promise that shadows break,
her voice the map that guides me home.

With her, I find the strength
to wrestle storms and rise again,
to carve hope from jagged stone,
to believe that even ruin can bloom.

And if my last words must be sung,
let them be hymns for her—
a melody etched in fading breath,
a vow carried beyond silence itself.

I will shield her through fire and frost,
stand unbroken against the world’s cruelty,
for the universe must bend and yield,
before it dares to dim her light.
