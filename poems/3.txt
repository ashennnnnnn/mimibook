My honey

Her hazel eyes are lanterns,
soft embers glowing in twilight's hush,
her hair, a river of brown silk,
falling like whispers across the day.

Her smile-

a sunrise breaking through clouded skies,
a quiet hymn that steadies the heart,
the kind of light that makes
ordinary hours bloom into miracles.

But if shadows dare to touch her,
if cruelty dares to breathe her name,
then let the heavens fracture,
let the oceans rise in molten rage.

I will summon storms to split the earth,
drag constellations into fire,
and crown the horizon with ruin-
for the world itself must fall,
if ever it conspires
to dim the radiance of her smile.
