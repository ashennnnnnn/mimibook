FOR YOU, ALWAYS

My Mimi

your eyes, a hazel shade,
make me forget the pain this life,
the feeling of threats
the radiant warmth of your mahogany hair
feels like the world is a cozily sphere

and as much as I could, I would for you
breaking my bones, cutting my wounds
anything to keep that one crescent intact
that decorates the face that keeps me in wraps

and for you my love, I'll be the tempest
that shatters all silence
and in your embrace,
I'll be the calm after the storm.

forever yours
