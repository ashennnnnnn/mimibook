this poem may be a little childish
cause you wake up the child inside my heart
and I want to let you know that, he's little and
he makes some mistakes, he's also dumb and aloof
but that doesn't mean that he doesnt have love
he has alot for you, and only you 
since that wedding aisle that smelled like caramel
that scent that reminded him of you awoke

awaiting a response- writing for eons and eons
he makes mistakes, fine, but he's willing to fix them
so give him your hand, just once, this time
and he will walk the path you lay for him
he will listen to everything, and using that tiny brain of his
he will do whatever he can, you his wife
