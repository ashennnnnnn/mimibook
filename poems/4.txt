Hidden Corners

I could’ve never known,
a child still lingered in shadowed halls,
quietly sketching dreams in dust,
waiting for laughter to call.

Then you arrived—
with a smile that bends the silence,
a spark that dances through the air,
warmth wrapping me like a secret blanket,
soft, unspoken, rare.

Your joy is a lantern,
pulling me from forgotten corners,
where echoes of innocence hid,
and suddenly—
I am playful again,
alive again,
whole again.

This aura you carry,
a mystery wrapped in comfort,
a melody stitched with light—
I hope I can give you everything,
the world, the stars,
and the endless delight
you’ve already given me tonight.
