The Last Breath and the Angel

Upon the shattered field he stands,
a mortal frame against the gods,
his breath a ragged hymn of defiance,
his hands blood‑worn from endless war.

The world itself bends to break him,
storms claw, mountains groan,
yet still he rises,
a lone flame against the abyss.

The silence of surrender whispers,
his knees falter,
his heart trembles at the edge of night,
and despair coils like a serpent near.

But then the heavens part,
an angel descends in silver light,
her wings a covenant of hope,
her gaze a balm upon his ruin.

She lifts him from the dust,
shares her wings with his weary soul,
and together they ascend,
beyond the grasp of gods,
beyond the chains of earth,
into an unknown world
where struggle becomes song,
and eternity begins anew.
